Pick Jev as a faction's commander to have a decision model make its calls. Every few seconds it is sent the map as that faction sees it and asked, for each idle army, which neighbouring region to move to (or to hold), and whether to spend gold on a new army. Answers it fails to give are filled in by the local heuristic and shown in orange.
Your key is sent only to the model endpoint and is forgotten when the tab closes. Calls are billed to it.
